## Challenge

Housing leaders face pressure to support supply and affordability, but headline house-price reporting does not explain whether pressure is sustained, how transaction activity changes the picture, or where policy and development teams should investigate further.

Your team will work as a multidisciplinary delivery team rather than as a group of people independently producing charts.

The engagement combines business analysis, data governance, data engineering, analytics, BI/decision support, assurance and specialist roles relevant to the project.

## Commissioned Decision

> **Which housing-market areas show the strongest evidence of sustained market and affordability pressure, and where should development, policy or investment teams investigate first?**

A successful submission must help structure or support this decision. A dashboard without governed definitions, reproducible data, analytical evidence and a defensible decision framework is not sufficient.

## Core Objective

Build a reproducible cross-functional solution that:

1. build a governed historical HPI analytical model;
2. standardise geography, period, property and buyer dimensions;
3. identify sustained rather than one-period price pressure;
4. analyse transaction and property-type context;
5. separate market-pressure evidence from true affordability claims;
6. develop transparent multi-indicator priority scenarios;
7. perform sensitivity analysis on look-back windows and weights;
8. build policy/development decision-support BI;
9. complete assurance, UAT and reproducible handover;

## Key Business Questions

- Which areas show sustained price growth?
- How do transactions and property type change the interpretation?
- How do buyer-type measures differ where available?
- Which areas look pressured across multiple indicators rather than price alone?
- What income, supply, planning or demographic evidence is still needed for a true affordability conclusion?

## Important Interpretation Rules

- House prices alone do not measure affordability.
- Do not mix incompatible geographic or buyer/property series.
- Do not present market-pressure outputs as investment advice.
- Expose every component and weight in any composite score.
- Clearly separate measured market evidence from affordability hypotheses.

## Agreed Success Standard

The engagement is considered successful only when:

- the approved frozen source can be identified and obtained;
- curated outputs can be regenerated from code/instructions;
- material exclusions and data-quality issues are quantified;
- headline KPIs reconcile to the curated analytical model;

- at least five headline dashboard measures reconcile to underlying data;

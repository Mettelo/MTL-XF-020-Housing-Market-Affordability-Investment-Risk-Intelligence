# Project Brief

## Client Commission
The board faces pressure to support housing supply and affordability but current reporting over-focuses on headline prices. Leadership cannot consistently separate structural price growth, transaction activity, property mix, buyer type and geographic variation when deciding where intervention or further investment analysis is justified.

The multidisciplinary team has been commissioned to answer:

> **Which housing-market areas show the strongest evidence of sustained affordability and market-pressure concerns, and where should development, policy or investment teams investigate first?**

## Primary Dataset
**UK House Price Index — full historical CSV**  
Publisher: HM Land Registry  
Official source: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/uk-house-price-index-data-downloads-june-2026

## Required Delivery Lifecycle
**Discovery → Requirements → Governance → Acquisition → Engineering → Data Quality → Analysis → Decision Framework → BI/Product → Assurance/UAT → Executive Recommendation → Handover**

## Cross-Functional Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Define housing-policy/investment decision, stakeholder requirements and acceptance criteria. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Govern geography, measure definitions, history, lineage and quality. |
| Data Engineer | Build reproducible HPI ingestion/transformation and analytical model. |
| Housing/Data Analyst | Analyse price, transaction, property/buyer and regional patterns. |
| BI Developer | Build housing-market decision-support product and governed KPIs. |
| Economist / Policy Analyst | Interpret market evidence within policy and economic context. |
| Data Scientist | Develop transparent scenarios/segmentation where justified. |
| Model Governance / Assurance | Challenge assumptions, proxies, validation and decision use. |

## Business Questions
1. Which geographies show sustained price pressure rather than a single-period increase?
2. How do property type, buyer type and transaction activity change the interpretation?
3. Which measures can reasonably support an affordability-pressure view, and what additional data would be needed for true affordability?
4. Which data-quality, geographic or historical-comparison issues could change the conclusion?
5. Where should policy, development or investment teams investigate first?
6. What management KPIs should be monitored after handover?

## Success Criteria
The solution should demonstrate traceable requirements, governed definitions and lineage, reproducible HPI preparation, defensible house-price trends, transaction activity, property type, buyer type, geographic variation, affordability-pressure proxies and intervention-prioritisation scenarios, explicit treatment of missing affordability context, transparent decision logic, useful BI, UAT/assurance evidence, executive recommendations and operational handover.

## Review Panel
Mettelo reviewers may challenge geographic comparisons, time-series handling, property/buyer definitions, use of affordability proxies, thresholds, model assumptions, recommendations and maintainability.

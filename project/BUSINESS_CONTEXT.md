# Business Context

## Simulated Client
**Confidential Regional Housing & Economic Development Board**

## Business Pressure
The board faces pressure to support housing supply and affordability but current reporting over-focuses on headline prices. Leadership cannot consistently separate structural price growth, transaction activity, property mix, buyer type and geographic variation when deciding where intervention or further investment analysis is justified.

## Executive Decision
**Which housing-market areas show the strongest evidence of sustained affordability and market-pressure concerns, and where should development, policy or investment teams investigate first?**

## Stakeholders
- **Director of Housing Strategy:** sponsor and decision owner.
- **Housing Development Lead:** tests development/investment relevance.
- **Policy/Economic Lead:** challenges interpretation and external context.
- **Data Owner/Steward:** owns definitions, lineage and quality challenge.
- **Finance/Investment stakeholder:** tests materiality and prioritisation.
- **Data & Analytics Lead:** owns technical quality and maintainability.
- **End users:** participate in UAT.

## Delivery Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Define housing-policy/investment decision, stakeholder requirements and acceptance criteria. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Govern geography, measure definitions, history, lineage and quality. |
| Data Engineer | Build reproducible HPI ingestion/transformation and analytical model. |
| Housing/Data Analyst | Analyse price, transaction, property/buyer and regional patterns. |
| BI Developer | Build housing-market decision-support product and governed KPIs. |
| Economist / Policy Analyst | Interpret market evidence within policy and economic context. |
| Data Scientist | Develop transparent scenarios/segmentation where justified. |
| Model Governance / Assurance | Challenge assumptions, proxies, validation and decision use. |

The intended outcome is a trusted evidence base for deciding where further housing intervention is warranted, not a league table of expensive places.

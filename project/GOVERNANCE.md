# Project Governance

This is a simulated Mettelo engagement using official public/open data. Participants must not imply a real confidential-client relationship.

## Data Governance
Document publisher, frozen source version, geography definitions, glossary, critical fields, ownership, lineage, DQ rules, transformations, metric ownership and limitations.

## Responsible Interpretation
House prices alone do not measure affordability, housing need or investment viability. Teams must distinguish price movement from income-based affordability, avoid causal claims and make missing socioeconomic context explicit.

Any scoring, segmentation or scenario is decision support. Teams must separate market evidence from policy, investment and professional judgement and document what additional income, supply, planning or demographic evidence would be required for stronger conclusions.

## GitHub Governance
The delivery repository is created under the Mettelo GitHub organisation. Add team members individually and use issues, branches, meaningful commits, PRs and reviews where practical. Never commit secrets or prohibited personal information.
